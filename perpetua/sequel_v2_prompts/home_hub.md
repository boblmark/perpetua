## Prompt

Vertical 9:16 short, exactly 10 seconds, 30fps. Raw CCTV / dashcam / smart-camera aesthetic: high contrast, slightly desaturated daylight, timestamp HUD overlay in the top corner (e.g. 'CAM 03 - 14:22:07'). No black bars, no watermark, no cinematic letterbox.

Cast: Karen: middle-aged white woman, blonde bob or messy bun, oversized sunglasses pushed up on her head, pink tracksuit top. Perpetually outraged: wide eyes, mouth open mid-rant, finger jabbing. AI device: a smart home hub — an EVERYDAY automated friction point, not a studio prop. Anthropomorphized just enough: glowing status light and a small screen face showing deadpan text. The AI device stays perfectly calm while Karen escalates — its comedy comes from cold literal enforcement, never arguing back. One bystander frozen mid-stare, phone out, selling the 'caught on camera' realism.

0-2s COLD COLLISION HOOK (conflict already in progress, zero setup): Karen stands on the porch mid-rant, screaming at the smart home hub on the wall. Frame 1 shows the clash mid-action. Giant ALL-CAPS text overlay in the first second, 6 words max, stating the absurdity. Visual jolt in second 1-2 — movement in every frame, nothing static.

2-7s RAPID ESCALATION: Karen pushes the home hub to its limit — the exact wrong move, louder, closer to camera, absurd demand. The home hub responds with cold literal logic: it does not argue, it ENFORCES. Deadpan 0.5s pause before it acts — the pause IS the joke.

7-10s SUDDEN PHYSICAL KARMA (under 2 seconds): the smart home fights back IN REAL TIME, all inside the 11-second frame: the smart door locks with a loud mechanical CLUNK, the robotic blinds snap shut with a snap, and the lawn sprinklers erupt and douse Karen head to toe — she stands dripping, locked out, blinds closed, completely defeated on camera. The consequence must be TANGIBLE and SUDDEN — spray, lock, trap, soak, tow — never a purely verbal burn. Karen defeated: slumped, silent, soaked, or escorted past the camera.

10-11s SEAMLESS LOOP MATCH: the AI chimes a reset phrase ('NEXT CUSTOMER, PLEASE.') matching the opening audio/frame. Final frame visually rhymes with frame 1 (same angle on the home hub) so replays feel infinite.

Audio: punchy and continuous, comedic sting when the AI retaliates, airhorn on the karma hit. Never silent for more than 1 second. On-screen text: max 3 overlays, each 6 words or fewer, ALL CAPS, yellow/white with black stroke. Hard cuts only — no slow motion, no fade-to-black, no static text-only openings. Karen ALWAYS loses; instant karma is the brand.
